2. Textos para colar — “Como esse app usará…”

Escritos em inglês de propósito: a fila de revisão é internacional. Cada um descreve o uso real, sem promessa que o código não cumpre.

instagram_business_basic

Comentou is a sales tool for Instagram professional accounts. After the account owner connects their own account through Business Login for Instagram, we use instagram_business_basic to identify which professional account was connected and to list that account's own media. Specifically we read: the account id and username, to label the connected account in the dashboard and to match incoming Direct messages to the right customer workspace; and the account's own posts and reels with their like and comment counts, so the owner can see which of their publications generated the most conversation. Without this permission we cannot tell whose account is connected, so no other feature can be attributed to the correct user. We do not read media from accounts other than the one the user explicitly connected, and we do not use hashtag or public content search.

instagram_business_manage_messages

Comentou helps a business owner answer the Direct messages they already receive on their own Instagram professional account, and decide which conversation to answer first. We use instagram_business_manage_messages to: read the account's Direct conversations and the latest message of each thread, in order to detect which people wrote to the business and were never answered; and to send replies to those people from inside our dashboard, on behalf of and at the explicit request of the account owner. The ranking shown to the owner is built only from this data — whether the last message is inbound, how long ago it arrived, and how much of Meta's 24-hour messaging window remains. No message is sent automatically without the owner configuring it or clicking send. Without this permission the product has no function: the entire value is prioritising and answering the owner's own inbox.

instagram_business_manage_comments só se for pedir

Comentou lets a business owner turn comments on their own posts into private conversations, which is the standard comment-to-DM flow. We use instagram_business_manage_comments to read comments left on the connected account's own media, to detect when a comment matches a keyword the owner configured (for example a product name), and to reply to that comment publicly and/or send the commenter a private reply, as configured by the owner. We only read comments on media belonging to the account that granted the permission. We do not read comments on third-party media and we do not aggregate comment data across accounts.

Um vídeo por permissão. Motivo nº 1 de rejeição: não aparecer a tela de consentimento.

Regra que derruba a maioria: o vídeo precisa mostrar o logout → login → tela de permissão do Instagram com os itens visíveis → o dado aparecendo no app. Se cortar a tela de consentimento, é rejeição automática.
  1. Comece deslogado. Mostre a tela de login.
  2. Faça login com o usuário de teste.
  3. Vá em Configurações → clique em Conectar Instagram.
  4. Pare na tela do Instagram e deixe visível por 3–4 segundos, com as permissões legíveis. Não corte.
  5. Autorize e volte ao app.
  6. Para basic: abra Resultados e mostre os posts com curtidas e comentários.
  7. Para manage_messages: abra Oportunidades, mostre as conversas e envie uma resposta de verdade. Depois abra o Mapa de Calor e mostre a fila priorizada.

6. O que muda quando aprovar

Hoje o app tem Standard Access: só contas com função de testador no app conseguem conectar. Por isso a sua conta precisou ser adicionada manualmente.

Com Advanced Access, qualquer conta profissional do Instagram conecta sozinha — que é o fluxo que você quer vender: o cliente cadastra, conecta, paga e usa, sem depender de você.

Não existe atalho. Pagamento, paywall e código não substituem essa aprovação: quem autoriza a leitura do Instagram de terceiros é a Meta, não o seu sistema.
