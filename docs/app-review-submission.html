2. Textos para colar — “Como esse app usará…”

Escritos em inglês de propósito: a fila de revisão é internacional. Cada um descreve o uso real, sem promessa que o código não cumpre.

instagram_business_basic

Comentou is a sales tool for Instagram professional accounts. After the account owner connects their own account through Business Login for Instagram, we use instagram_business_basic to identify which professional account was connected and to list that account's own media. Specifically we read: the account id and username, to label the connected account in the dashboard and to match incoming Direct messages to the right customer workspace; and the account's own posts and reels with their like and comment counts, so the owner can see which of their publications generated the most conversation. Without this permission we cannot tell whose account is connected, so no other feature can be attributed to the correct user. We do not read media from accounts other than the one the user explicitly connected, and we do not use hashtag or public content search.

instagram_business_manage_messages

Comentou helps a business owner answer the Direct messages they already receive on their own Instagram professional account, and decide which conversation to answer first. We use instagram_business_manage_messages to: read the account's Direct conversations and the latest message of each thread, in order to detect which people wrote to the business and were never answered; and to send replies to those people from inside our dashboard, on behalf of and at the explicit request of the account owner. The ranking shown to the owner is built only from this data — whether the last message is inbound, how long ago it arrived, and how much of Meta's 24-hour messaging window remains. No message is sent automatically without the owner configuring it or clicking send. Without this permission the product has no function: the entire value is prioritising and answering the owner's own inbox.

instagram_business_manage_comments só se for pedir

Comentou lets a business owner turn comments on their own posts into private conversations, which is the standard comment-to-DM flow. We use instagram_business_manage_comments to read comments left on the connected account's own media, to detect when a comment matches a keyword the owner configured (for example a product name), and to reply to that comment publicly and/or send the commenter a private reply, as configured by the owner. We only read comments on media belonging to the account that granted the permission. We do not read comments on third-party media and we do not aggregate comment data across accounts.

6. O que muda quando aprovar

Hoje o app tem Standard Access: só contas com função de testador no app conseguem conectar. Por isso a sua conta precisou ser adicionada manualmente.

Com Advanced Access, qualquer conta profissional do Instagram conecta sozinha — que é o fluxo que você quer vender: o cliente cadastra, conecta, paga e usa, sem depender de você.

Não existe atalho. Pagamento, paywall e código não substituem essa aprovação: quem autoriza a leitura do Instagram de terceiros é a Meta, não o seu sistema.

7. Marketing API — Access Tier

Leia antes de enviar. Procurei no código inteiro do Comentou (todos os .ts e .tsx) por qualquer chamada de Marketing API — act_, adaccount, adcreative, ads_, insights/ads. Zero resultados. O app não faz nenhuma chamada de anúncios hoje.

O caso de uso “Criar e gerenciar anúncios” está ativado no painel (é ele que puxa ads_management, ads_read, business_management e boa parte das 13 permissões extras que estamos removendo da submissão). Pedir tier de uma API que o app não chama é exatamente o padrão de rejeição que estamos corrigindo — e uma rejeição aqui atrasa a aprovação do Instagram, que é a que destrava a venda.

Minha recomendação: desativar o caso de uso de anúncios agora, aprovar o Instagram, e voltar ao Marketing API depois que a integração existir de fato. O texto abaixo está pronto para quando esse dia chegar — ou para hoje, se você decidir enviar mesmo assim. Ele é honesto: descreve leitura para atribuição, não criação de anúncios. Só não afirme que já está no ar antes de estar.

Campo: “How does your app use the Marketing API?”

Cole em inglês. É o campo principal do pedido de tier.

Comentou is a Brazilian SaaS that helps small businesses turn Instagram comments and direct messages into sales conversations. Our users are the advertisers themselves: each customer connects their own Instagram professional account and their own ad account. We never operate ads for accounts outside that explicit connection.

We use the Marketing API in READ-ONLY mode, for attribution and reporting. We do not create, edit, duplicate, pause, or spend on ads, and we do not expose any ad-creation surface to our users.

Endpoints we call:

1. GET /me/adaccounts — so the customer can pick which of their own ad accounts to link to their Comentou workspace.

2. GET /act_{ad_account_id}/ads and /adcreatives — to enumerate the creatives the advertiser is currently running, including dark posts, via effective_object_story_id and effective_instagram_media_id. This is the reason we need the Marketing API at all: comments and DMs generated by an unpublished ad creative never appear under the account's own media on the Instagram API, so today our users see the lead land in their inbox with no way to tell which campaign produced it.

3. GET /act_{ad_account_id}/insights at ad level, fields spend, impressions and actions — to attach a cost to the leads we already rank, so the customer can see cost per conversation started per campaign.

We read this data once per hour per connected account and store only the aggregate figures needed for the reports described above. We do not resell, share, or combine this data across customers.

Value to the advertiser: today a small business has two disconnected views. Ads Manager tells them what they spent; the Instagram inbox tells them who wrote. Comentou joins the two, so the person answering direct messages knows which conversations were paid for and which were organic, and spends Meta's 24-hour messaging window on the leads that actually cost money.

Business model: monthly subscription paid by the business that owns the account. We are not an ad network, a reseller, or an agency operating third-party ad accounts.

Campo: “Which ad objects will your app create or manage?”

None. Our integration is read-only. We do not create or manage campaigns, ad sets, ads, creatives, audiences, or budgets. We read ads, ad creatives, and ad-level insights for attribution reporting only.

Campo: “How many ad accounts do you expect to access?”

One ad account per paying customer, connected by the customer themselves. We expect fewer than 100 connected ad accounts in the first year. Call volume is roughly 4 requests per connected account per hour.

Se o formulário pedir justificativa de volume (Standard → Advanced Tier)

Development Tier's rate limit is applied per app, not per ad account, so it is consumed by the total number of connected customers rather than by how heavily any one customer uses the product. Our polling is deliberately light — about 4 calls per account per hour — but it scales linearly with customers, and Development Tier stops being sufficient well before our own capacity does. We are requesting the next tier to keep hourly attribution working as customers are onboarded, not to increase per-customer call frequency.
